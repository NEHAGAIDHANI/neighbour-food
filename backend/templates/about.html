Sharing Plates, Building Community

NeighborFood connects local home cooks with hungry neighbors.

Our Mission

We believe that the best food isn't found in restaurants, but in the kitchens of our neighbors. Our project aims to reduce food waste and bring people together through the joy of home-cooked meals.

How It Works

  • Cooks: List your extra portions.
  • Neighbors: Browse meals nearby.
  • Connect: Pick up fresh food and meet a neighbor!
